Slide 05 • Core Research & Systems Challenges

Overcoming the 4 Critical Engineering Hurdles

NLP • State Machines • MLOps • Security
01
Schema Complexity

Text-to-SQL on 71 Tables & 91 Foreign Keys

Dumping 71 ERP tables into an LLM prompt causes context overflow and hallucinated JOIN paths.

Our Engineering Solution

Dynamic Vector Schema Scoping prunes 71 tables into 2–4 target relations while preserving full FK JOIN fidelity.

02
Agent Determinism

Breaking Unbounded ReAct Loops

Standard autonomous agents get stuck in infinite tool-calling loops or execute unapproved state mutations.

Our Engineering Solution

Deterministic Cyclic State Graphs (LangGraph) with bounded retries (max 3) and persistent MemorySaver checkpoints.

03
Statistical MLOps

Intermittent & Asymmetric Demand Forecasting

Retail SKU sales exhibit zero-inflated intermittent demand and non-Gaussian variance during local holidays.

Our Engineering Solution

Non-parametric LightGBM Quantile Regression ($\alpha \in \{0.025, 0.5, 0.975\}$) with calendar holiday weights & EWMA velocity.

04
Multi-Tenant Security

Cryptographic Multi-Tenant Isolation

Competing merchants share database infrastructure; a single missing WHERE clause could leak rival financial data.

Our Engineering Solution

PostgreSQL 16 Kernel Row-Level Security (RLS) bound to verified JWT tenant_id session contexts.
